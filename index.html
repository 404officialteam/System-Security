Your Essential Data Rights

Right to Know: Discover what personal information is being collected.

Right to Access: Request copies of your personal data records.

Right to Delete: Demand removal of your stored data ("Right to be Forgotten").

Right to Opt-Out: Stop companies from selling or sharing your information.

Data Portability: Transfer your data seamlessly between services.

How Hackers Target You

Phishing: Deceptive messages tricking you into revealing sensitive credentials.

Malware & Ransomware: Malicious software that infects devices or encrypts files for ransom.

Man-in-the-Middle: Eavesdropping on communications over unsecured public Wi-Fi.

Social Engineering: Psychological manipulation to trick users into security slips.

Critical Stat: Over 95% of all successful cybersecurity breaches are caused by human error!

Don't Take the Bait!

Phishing is the #1 vector for credential theft and network intrusion globally.

Common Attack Types

  • Email Phishing: Mass deceptive emails imitating official entities.
  • Spear Phishing: Highly targeted attacks tailored to a specific victim.
  • Smishing & Vishing: Fraudulent SMS texts or phone impersonation.

🚩 Key Phishing Red Flags

  • • Sense of extreme urgency ("Account Suspended Immediately!")
  • • Mismatched or unusual sender email domains
  • • Generic greetings & grammatical errors
  • • Unexpected attachments or suspicious external links

The First Line of Defense

✕ Common Mistakes

  • Reusing same password across multiple sites
  • Using simple strings like "123456" or "password"
  • Including personal info (birthdays, pet names)
  • Writing passwords on physical sticky notes

✓ Best Practices

  • Use a trusted Password Manager (e.g. Bitwarden)
  • Create long passphrases (4+ random words)
  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication everywhere
  • Change credentials immediately after a breach

Double Lock Your Accounts

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) combines two distinct factors: Something you know (Password) + Something you have (Phone/Key).

HIGHEST

Hardware Key

YubiKey / FIDO2 security key. Immune to phishing.

RECOMMENDED

Auth App

Google / Microsoft / Authy time-based codes (TOTP).

VULNERABLE

SMS Text

6-digit code sent via phone network. Risk of SIM swap.

BASIC

Email Code

Code sent via email. Weak if email is compromised.

Warning on SIM Swapping: Hackers trick phone carriers into transferring your phone number to their SIM card to hijack SMS 2FA codes. Always prefer Authenticator Apps over SMS!

Beyond Technology: Human Element

Social Engineering Tactics

  • Pretexting: Inventing a scenario to trick victim into disclosing info.
  • Baiting: Leaving infected USB drives in public or offering fake downloads.
  • Quid Pro Quo: Requesting credentials in exchange for a service/gift.
  • Tailgating: Following authorized personnel into restricted areas.

Every Click Leaves a Trail

Your Digital Footprint is the permanent online trail of data created by your internet activity.

ACTIVE FOOTPRINT

Data you intentionally share (social posts, comments, uploaded photos, online forms).

PASSIVE FOOTPRINT

Data collected without direct awareness (IP address, browsing history, location logs).

Why It Matters

Employers audit online profiles during hiring • Advertisers construct psychological profiles • Cybercriminals use exposed info for identity theft.

4 Types of Digital Footprints

Protect Your Online Persona

Social Media Risks

  • Oversharing real-time locations, home address & routines
  • Accepting connection requests from unknown/fake profiles
  • Public posts harvested by data brokers and employers
  • Targeted social engineering using personal background info

Golden Rule: Share content assuming your grandmother, your future employer, and a hacker are all reading it!

Secure Your Digital Doorways

Public Wi-Fi Danger Zone

Public hotspots are unencrypted. Hackers on the same network can intercept unencrypted data streams ("Evil Twin" networks & packet sniffing).

Always use a VPN: Encrypts all device traffic on public networks.

Ensure HTTPS: Look for lock icons on address bars before typing data.

Home Router Security

  • Change factory default admin credentials immediately
  • Use WPA3 encryption standard (or WPA2-AES minimum)
  • Disable WPS (Wi-Fi Protected Setup) to prevent brute-force attacks

Securing All Your Deveices

Computers & Laptops

  • OS Updates: Install patches immediately to fix critical security flaws.
  • Disk Encryption: Enable BitLocker (Win) or FileVault (Mac) for loss protection.
  • Firewall & Antivirus: Block unauthorized network requests and scan files.
  • Screen Lock: Set auto-lock timeout under 3 minutes.

Smartphones & Tablets

  • App Stores Only: Download apps exclusively from Google Play / Apple Store.
  • Review Permissions: Revoke contacts, camera, or location access for basic apps.
  • Biometric / PIN: Enforce strong 6-digit PIN or biometrics.
  • Enable "Find My": Allows remote wipe if device is lost or stolen.

Universal Security Rule: Update Software → Update OS → Update Firmware!

The Internet of Things

IoT Devices: Smart TVs, security cameras, smart speakers, thermostats, and wearables.

Why IoT Devices are High-Risk

  • Shipped with weak or hardcoded factory passwords
  • Rarely receive long-term security firmware updates
  • Can be recruited into massive Botnets (e.g. Mirai DDoS attacks)
  • Always-on microphones & cameras create privacy exposure

IoT Hardening Best Practices

Isolate IoT devices onto a separate Guest Network VLAN.
